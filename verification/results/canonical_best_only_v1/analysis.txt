Method Notes
- Parameter stability is not tested because neighboring combinations were intentionally excluded.
- Fixed and expanding walk-forward use the same fixed combo, so their validation-year results
  are identical; no fold-specific parameter selection occurs.
- Purged/embargoed CV is recorded as fixed_single_combo. With no parameter fitting, purge and
  embargo do not change model selection; the useful output is the calendar-block performance.
- Data quality is marked degraded, but unresolved corporate-action count is zero.

Decision
- The result is strong enough to proceed to a paper account without further parameter search.
- It is not strong enough to justify real capital yet: full-stress MDD reaches -67.68%, annual
  QQQ underperformance occurs in 4 of 11 years, and the degraded data warning needs an audit.
- Paper trading should keep this configuration frozen and compare live signals, intended orders,
  actual simulated fills, fees, slippage, and position identity against the canonical backtest.
